# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Hospital Emergency Room (ER) operations using Business Analysis, SQL, and Excel/Power BI techniques.

The objective of this project is to improve hospital efficiency, reduce patient waiting time, and enhance patient satisfaction through data-driven insights and dashboard visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The hospital emergency room faces challenges in managing patient flow, monitoring waiting times, and maintaining patient satisfaction.

Due to limited operational visibility, it becomes difficult for management to make timely and effective decisions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor patient volume trends
- Reduce patient waiting time
- Improve patient satisfaction
- Support data-driven decision-making
- Identify operational inefficiencies

---

# 📊 Scope of Analysis

- Patient volume analysis
- Wait time monitoring
- Satisfaction score analysis
- Peak hour identification
- Emergency room performance tracking

---

# 🧩 Business Analysis Process

- Requirement Gathering
- BRD & FRD Documentation
- As-Is & To-Be Process Analysis
- Wireframing
- UML / Process Flow Design
- Dashboard Planning
- KPI Identification

The dataset includes emergency room patient visit information such as:

# 📈 KPI Metrics

# 💡 Key Insights

- Peak days experience higher patient volume
- Increased load causes longer wait times
- Satisfaction decreases during busy periods
- Operational bottlenecks affect efficiency

---

# 🚀 Recommendations

- Increase staff during peak hours
- Improve patient flow management
- Optimize scheduling process
- Enhance service quality monitoring
